🐕 Border Collie

$300

The Border Collie originated along the border of Scotland and England, where it was bred to herd sheep across rugged hills and farmland. Developed over hundreds of years, this breed became famous for its incredible intelligence, speed, and unmatched herding ability. Their famous "herding eye" allows them to control livestock with remarkable precision. Today, Border Collies are not only exceptional working dogs but also excel in agility, obedience, search-and-rescue, and make loyal companions for active families.
