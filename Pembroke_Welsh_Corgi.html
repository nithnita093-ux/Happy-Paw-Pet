🐶 Pembroke Welsh Corgi

$450

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi originated in Wales over 1,000 years ago and was originally bred to herd cattle, sheep, and horses. Despite their short legs, they are energetic and hardworking herding dogs. Corgis became famous worldwide as the favorite breed of Queen Elizabeth II, helping them gain popularity as loving family pets. Today, they are admired for their intelligence, cheerful personality, and adorable appearance.
