The German Shepherd originated in Germany during the late 19th century. The breed was developed by Captain Max von Stephanitz, who aimed to create the perfect working dog. German Shepherds were originally used for herding sheep, but their intelligence, courage, and loyalty soon made them popular as police dogs, military dogs, search-and-rescue dogs, and family companions. Today, they are one of the most respected and versatile dog breeds in the world.
