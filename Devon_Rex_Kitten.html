📖 Breed History

The Devon Rex kitten is a super unique cat breed known for its curly fur, huge ears, and elf-like face 👽✨. It’s one of the most playful and “mischievous cute” cats you can find.

❤️ Personality

  • Extremely playful and energetic ⚡.
  • Very curious and naughty (in a cute way 😆).
  • Loves climbing, jumping, exploring.
  • Very social and people-oriented ❤️.
  • Often follows owner everywhere like a shadow.
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • High-protein cat food
  • oys and short indoor games are enough
  • Gentle brushing once a week is enough
  • 🌡️ Sensitive to cold (thin coat).
  • Should not be underfed (they burn energy fast)

💉 Health Information

  • Vaccinated ✔
  • Dewormed ✔
  • Healthy ✔
  • Microchipped ✔
