📖 Breed History

The Papillon Puppy is a small, elegant toy breed known for its distinctive butterfly-shaped ears, lively personality, and remarkable intelligence. Despite their tiny size, Papillon puppies are energetic, fearless, and highly trainable. They enjoy learning tricks, playing games, and spending time with their families, making them wonderful companions for both individuals and families.

🍖 Care Guide

  • Feed a high-quality puppy food formulated for large breeds.
  • Feed 3–4 meals per day until about 6 months old, then reduce to 2 meals daily.
  • Always provide fresh, clean water.
  • Avoid overfeeding to support healthy bone and joint development.
  • Brush 2–3 times per week.
