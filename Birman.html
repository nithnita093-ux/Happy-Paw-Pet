📖 Breed History

The Birman, also known as the "Sacred Cat of Burma," is a medium-to-large cat breed admired for its striking blue eyes, silky semi-long coat, and distinctive white "gloves" on each paw. Birmans are gentle, affectionate, and sociable cats that enjoy spending time with their families. They are calm yet playful, making them excellent companions for homes with children and other pets.
