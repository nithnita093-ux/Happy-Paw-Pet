📖 Breed History

The Birman kitten, also known as the "Sacred Cat of Burma," is a beautiful and affectionate cat breed famous for its deep blue eyes, silky coat, and distinctive white paws, often called "white gloves." 🐾💙

❤️ Personality

  • Extremely active and playful ⚡
  • gentle and affectionate ❤️.
  • famous for beautiful blue eyes and white "gloves" 🐾.
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Enjoys following its owner around 🐾.
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • Needs light to moderate playtime
  • oys and short indoor games are enough
  • Brush 2–3 times per week.
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they get “round” fast).
