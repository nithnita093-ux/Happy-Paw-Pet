📖 Breed History

The Norwegian Forest kitten is a large, fluffy, and gentle cat breed known for its thick waterproof coat, bushy tail, and strong build. Originally adapted to the cold forests of Norway, it is often called the "Wegie" by cat lovers.

❤️ Personality

  • Gentle and affectionate ❤️.
  • Gets along well with children and other pets.
  • Independent yet enjoys human companionship.
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Quiet (not very vocal 💤)
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • Fresh water should always be available.
  • ⚖️ Can become overweight if not exercised regularly.
  • Brush once a week
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they get “round” fast).

💉 Health Information

  • Vaccinated ✔
  • Dewormed ✔
  • Healthy ✔
  • Microchipped ✔
