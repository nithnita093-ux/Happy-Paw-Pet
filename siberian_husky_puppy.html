📖 Breed History

The Siberian Husky Puppy is an energetic, intelligent, and friendly working dog known for its striking blue or multicolored eyes, thick double coat, and wolf-like appearance. Originally bred to pull sleds across snowy terrain, Husky puppies are playful, curious, and love adventure. They require plenty of exercise, early training, and socialization to become well-behaved adult dogs.

🍖 Care Guide

  • Feed a high-quality puppy food formulated for large breeds.
  • Feed 3–4 meals per day until about 6 months old, then reduce to 2 meals daily.
  • Always provide fresh, clean water.
  • Avoid overfeeding to support healthy bone and joint development.
  • Brush 2–3 times per week.
