📖 Breed History

The Turkish Angora kitten is an elegant, intelligent, and graceful cat breed known for its silky coat and “dancer-like” body movement ✨. It is one of the oldest natural cat breeds.

❤️ Personality

  • White (most famous 🤍).
  • Very intelligent and curious 🧠.
  • Intelligent and curious.
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Playful and energetic ⚡.
  • Strong personality (not shy).

🍖 Care Guide

  • Needs energy for active lifestyle.
  • Needs balanced meals (they love eating 😆).
  • Brush once a week
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they get “round” fast).
