The Dachshund originated in Germany over 600 years ago and was originally bred to hunt badgers, rabbits, and other burrowing animals. Their long body, short legs, and fearless personality made them excellent hunters capable of digging into tunnels. The name "Dachshund" means "badger dog" in German. Today, they are cherished family pets known for their playful spirit, loyalty, and unique appearance.
