📖 Breed History

The Sphynx is a unique, hairless cat breed known for its wrinkled skin, large ears, and outgoing personality. Despite their lack of fur, Sphynx cats are warm to the touch and highly affectionate. They thrive on human attention, love to cuddle for warmth, and are one of the most social and playful cat breeds, making them excellent companions for families and individuals alike.

❤️ Personality

  • Extremely affectionate ❤️
  • Highly intelligent 🧠
  • Playful
  • Social and interactive
  • Loves attention
  • Perfect indoor pet

🍖 Care Guide

  • Brush once a week
  • Provide fresh water
  • Feed high-quality, protein-rich cat food.
  • Vaccinations every year
  • Sphynx cats have a fast metabolism and may eat more than other breeds.
