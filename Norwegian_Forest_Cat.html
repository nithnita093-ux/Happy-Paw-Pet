📖 Breed History

The Norwegian Forest Cat is a large, powerful, and fluffy cat breed that originated in the cold forests of Norway. Famous for its thick waterproof double coat, bushy tail, and strong climbing ability, this breed is both rugged and elegant. Norwegian Forest Cats are affectionate, intelligent, and independent, enjoying family companionship while also appreciating their personal space.
