📖 Breed History

The Bengal is a striking, athletic cat breed known for its wild appearance and leopard-like spots or marbled coat. Despite their exotic look, Bengals are affectionate, highly intelligent, and energetic companions. They are curious, playful, and love climbing, exploring, and interactive games, making them ideal for active households.

❤️ Personality

  • Friendly
  • Highly intelligent 🧠
  • Confident and fearless
  • Social and interactive
  • Good with children
  • Perfect indoor pet

🍖 Care Guide

  • Brush once a week
  • Provide fresh water
  • Feed high-quality, protein-rich cat food.
  • Vaccinations every year
  • Avoid excessive treats to maintain a healthy weight.
