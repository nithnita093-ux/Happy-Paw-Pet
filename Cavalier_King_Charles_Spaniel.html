🍖 Care Guide

  • Feed a high-quality puppy food formulated for large breeds.
  • Feed 3–4 meals per day until about 6 months old, then reduce to 2 meals daily.
  • Always provide fresh, clean water.
  • Avoid overfeeding to support healthy bone and joint development.
  • Brush 2–3 times per week.
