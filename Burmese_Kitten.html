📖 Breed History

The Burmese kitten is a small, muscular, and very affectionate cat known for being extremely people-oriented. It often acts like a “dog in a cat body” because it loves attention and following its owner everywhere ❤️.

❤️ Personality

  • Extremely affectionate and loving ❤️.
  • Very social (hates being alone).
  • Intelligent and curious.
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Quiet (not very vocal 💤)
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • Enjoys play sessions and toys.
  • Needs balanced meals (they love eating 😆).
  • Brush once a week
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they get “round” fast).

💉 Health Information

  • Vaccinated ✔
  • Dewormed ✔
  • Healthy ✔
  • Microchipped ✔
