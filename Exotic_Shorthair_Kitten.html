📖 Breed History

The Exotic Shorthair kitten is often called the “lazy Persian” 🧸 because it looks like a Persian cat but with much shorter, easier-to-care-for fur.It is one of the most popular calm and cute teddy-bear-style cats.

❤️ Personality

  • Very calm and relaxed.
  • Sweet and affectionate ❤️.
  • Quiet (rarely meows loudly)
  • Loves sitting next to owner.
  • Not very active (compared to Abyssinian).
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • 😮 Breathing difficulties (heat sensitivity)
  • oys and short indoor games are enough
  • Brush once a week
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid obesity (they are naturally “round”).

  • Vaccinated ✔
