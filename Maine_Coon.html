The Maine Coon is one of the oldest natural cat breeds in North America, originating in the state of Maine, USA. Known as the "Gentle Giant," this breed was originally kept on farms and ships because of its excellent hunting skills and ability to control rodents. Over time, Maine Coons became popular for their impressive size, thick water-resistant coats, and friendly personalities. Today, they are one of the most beloved family cats around the world.
