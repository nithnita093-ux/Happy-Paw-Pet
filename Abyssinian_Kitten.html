📖 Breed History

The Abyssinian kitten is one of the most active, smart, and energetic cat breeds. It has a wild, elegant look and a personality that is always curious and playful.

❤️ Personality

  • Extremely active and playful ⚡
  • Loves high places (shelves, cat trees 🪜)
  • Independent (not overly clingy)
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Quiet (not very vocal 💤)
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • Needs light to moderate playtime
  • oys and short indoor games are enough
  • Brush once a week
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they get “round” fast).
