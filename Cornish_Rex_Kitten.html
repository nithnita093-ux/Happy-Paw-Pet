📖 Breed History

The Cornish Rex kitten is a very unique cat with a soft curly coat, long legs, and an “alien/elf-like” face 👽✨. It is one of the most active and playful cat breeds in the world.

❤️ Personality

  • Extremely playful and energetic ⚡.
  • Extremely intelligent 🧠.
  • Can act like a “clown cat” (always entertaining).
  • Very social and people-oriented ❤️.
  • Often follows owner everywhere like a shadow.
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • High-protein cat food
  • oys and short indoor games are enough
  • Gentle brushing once a week is enough
  • 🌡️ Sensitive to cold (thin coat).
  • Should not be underfed (they burn energy fast)

💉 Health Information

  • Vaccinated ✔
  • Dewormed ✔
  • Healthy ✔
  • Microchipped ✔
