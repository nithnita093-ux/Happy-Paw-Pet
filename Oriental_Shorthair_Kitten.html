📖 Breed History

The Oriental Shorthair kitten is a super sleek, talkative, and energetic cat that looks very elegant and “model-like ✨”. It is closely related to the Siamese but comes in many colors and patterns.

  • Extremely active and playful ⚡
  • super smart and social 🧠.
  • very talkative and interactive 🗣️.
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Quiet (not very vocal 💤)
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • Needs enough calories for energy.
  • Toys and short indoor games are enough
  • Brush once a week
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid underfeeding due to high activity.
