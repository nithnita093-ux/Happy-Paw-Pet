The Poodle originated in Germany over 400 years ago and was originally bred as a water retriever, helping hunters retrieve ducks from lakes and rivers. The breed later became extremely popular in France, where it was refined into the elegant and intelligent companion we know today. Poodles are famous for their curly coats, exceptional intelligence, and friendly personalities. They come in three sizes: Standard, Miniature, and Toy Poodles.

🍖 Care Guide

  • Feed high-quality dog food twice a day.
  • Provide fresh, clean water at all times

  • Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
