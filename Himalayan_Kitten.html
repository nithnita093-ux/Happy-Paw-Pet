📖 Breed History

The Himalayan kitten is a super fluffy, elegant cat known for its long silky coat, flat face, and deep blue eyes 👀💙. It looks like a mix between a Persian and Siamese cat.

❤️ Personality

  • Very calm and gentle.
  • Sweet and affectionate ❤️.
  • Likes quiet environments.
  • Enjoys cuddling and relaxing.
  • Not very active (lazy cuddle cat type 😴).
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • Face cleaning is important (tear stains).
  • High-quality cat food
  • Needs brushing daily or every other day.
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they become very “round” 😄).

💉 Health Information

  • Vaccinated ✔
  • Dewormed ✔
  • Healthy ✔
