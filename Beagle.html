The Beagle originated in England and has been a popular hunting dog for centuries. Originally bred to track rabbits and other small game using its excellent sense of smell, the Beagle is now one of the world's most beloved family pets. Their cheerful personality, intelligence, and compact size make them wonderful companions for families and individuals alike.
