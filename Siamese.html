📖 Breed History

The Siamese cat originated in Phillipin, formerly known as Siam, where it was considered a sacred and royal companion. This elegant breed has been admired for centuries for its striking blue eyes, sleek body, and affectionate personality. Today, Siamese cats are one of the world's most popular and recognizable breeds.

❤️ Personality

  • Affectionate and loving
  • Loyal
  • Independent
  • Extremely social and people-oriented

🍖 Care Guide

  • Brush the coat once a week to remove loose hair.
  • Feed a balanced, high-quality diet.
  • Premium cat food
  • Schedule routine veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
  • Spend time playing and interacting with them every day, as Siamese cats crave attention.
