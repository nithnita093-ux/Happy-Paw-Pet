📖 Breed History

The Flemish Giant Bunny is one of the largest rabbit breeds in the world. Despite its impressive size, it is often called the "Gentle Giant" because of its calm, affectionate, and friendly personality.

❤️ Personality

  • Intelligent and curious 🧠.
  • Active and energetic ⚡.
  • Enjoys human companionship.
  • Loyal once it trusts its owner ❤️.
  • Quiet (not very vocal 💤)
  • Prefers gentle handling because of its large size.

🍖 Care Guide

  • Needs 3–4 hours of supervised exercise outside its enclosure each day.
  • Do not bathe rabbits unless instructed by a veterinarian.
  • Brush 1–2 times per week.
  • 🌾 Unlimited fresh hay (Timothy hay for adults; Alfalfa hay for young rabbits).
  • 🥬 Fresh leafy vegetables daily.
  • 🥕 Rabbit pellets in appropriate portions
  • 💧 Fresh, clean water available at all times.
  • 🍎 Fruit should only be given occasionally as a treat.

💉 Health Information

  • Vaccinated ✔
  • Dewormed ✔
  • Healthy ✔
  • Microchipped ✔
