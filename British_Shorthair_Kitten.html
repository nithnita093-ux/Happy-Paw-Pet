📖 Breed History

The British Shorthair kitten is one of the most popular cat breeds in the world, known for its round face, chubby cheeks, and super plush “teddy bear” fur 🧸

❤️ Personality

  • cute like a teddy bear 🧸
  • Playful
  • Independent (not overly clingy)
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Quiet (not very vocal 💤)
  • Good for peaceful homes or apartments

🍖 Care Guide

  • Needs light to moderate playtime
  • oys and short indoor games are enough
  • Brush 1–2 times per week.
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they get “round” fast).
