📖 Breed History

The Persian cat is one of the oldest and most beloved cat breeds in the world. Originating in Persia (modern-day Iran), these elegant cats were introduced to Europe during the 1600s and quickly became favorites among royalty and nobles. Known for their luxurious long coats, round faces, and calm personalities, Persian cats are now one of the most popular companion cats worldwide.

🍖 Care Guide

  • Sleeping in cozy beds.
  • Quiet indoor play.
  • Walk or exercise for at least 1 hour daily.
  • Brush the coat 2–3 times a week to reduce shedding.
  • Groom professionally every few months if needed.
