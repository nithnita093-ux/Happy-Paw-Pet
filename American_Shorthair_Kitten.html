📖 Breed History

The American Shorthair kitten is a friendly, easy-going, and adaptable cat breed known for its strong build, beautiful tabby patterns, and excellent health. It is one of the best cat breeds for first-time owners and families.

❤️ Personality

  • Extremely active and playful ⚡
  • riendly and affectionate ❤️.
  • Independent (not overly clingy)
  • Enjoys sitting near you, not always on you
  • Quiet (not very vocal 💤)
  • Enjoys watching birds from windows 🪟.

🍖 Care Guide

  • Regular nail trimming and ear cleaning are recommended.
  • 🦷 Regular dental care helps prevent oral disease.
  • Brush once a week
  • High-protein cat food.
  • Avoid overfeeding (they get “round” fast).
