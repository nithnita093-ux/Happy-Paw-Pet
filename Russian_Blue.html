📖 Breed History

The Russian Blue is an elegant, medium-sized cat breed known for its plush blue-gray coat, vivid emerald-green eyes, and graceful appearance. They are intelligent, gentle, and loyal cats that form strong bonds with their owners. Although they may be shy around strangers, Russian Blues are affectionate, playful, and devoted to their families, making them excellent indoor companions.

❤️ Personality

  • Friendly
  • Highly intelligent 🧠
  • Confident and fearless
  • Quiet
  • Independent but loving
  • Perfect indoor pet

🍖 Care Guide

  • Brush once a week
  • Provide fresh water
  • Feed high-quality, protein-rich cat food.
  • Vaccinations every year
  • Avoid overfeeding, as Russian Blues can gain weight if inactive.
